CompanyIntelBot

The crawler behind a company data service that is being built. If it visited your site, this page says what it does and how to stop it.

How to recognise it

Every request carries this User-Agent, with a link to this page:

CompanyIntelBot/1.0 (+this page's address)

What it fetches

  • Public job board APIs that applicant tracking systems publish for this purpose (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Recruitee, Personio, Workable), about once a day per board.
  • A few public pages of a company's own website: the home page, pages such as About, Products, Pricing and Careers, robots.txt and sitemap.xml. Never pages behind a login. Each visit has a fixed cap (requests, not counting robots.txt):
    • a company profile: at most 8;
    • looking for a company's job board: at most 15;
    • a careers page with no job board API: the page and at most 20 job pages, once a day.
  • Public DNS records of the company's domain (MX and TXT), to tell which services a company uses.

It does not fetch LinkedIn, Indeed or Glassdoor, does not run JavaScript, does not submit forms, and does not follow links to other sites.

How it behaves

  • It obeys robots.txt, under the name CompanyIntelBot (and *).
  • It sends at most one request per second to any one host, backs off on 429 and 503, and honours Retry-After.

What it keeps

  • Facts about a company (what it does, its products, links to its own pages and accounts, the services it uses) and its job postings. No lists of people, and no page text.
  • Job postings as the company published them, with email addresses and phone numbers removed before they are saved. A posting's text can still name people, for example a recruiter, because the company wrote it that way.
  • When a language model is used, the text of the pages above is sent to its provider (Anthropic) to write a short description of the company. Only the description is kept.

How to stop it

Add this to your site's robots.txt; it takes effect within a day:

User-agent: CompanyIntelBot
Disallow: /

To have your company or job board left out entirely, contact us (below). We delete everything stored for it, its postings and their history included, and it is not collected again. Copies in our backups are gone within 12 weeks.

For anything else, contact us too.
